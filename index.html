FAQ

Frequently asked questions

If you still have questions, they're probably answered right here.

Do I need prior experience in 3D?+

Depends on the course. Some start from zero, others go deeper for artists who already have a foundation. The level is on each card.

What software will I need?+

It varies per course and is listed before you buy. Across the school: ZBrush, Blender, Substance Painter, Houdini, TopoGun 3 and Marmoset Toolbag.

Is there a deadline to finish the courses?+

No. Access is lifetime. Study at your own pace and come back whenever you want.

Are the courses in Portuguese or English?+

Each card shows the language. The ZBrush courses, for example, are in English with Portuguese subtitles.

Will I be able to build a portfolio with these courses?+

Yes. Every course is project-driven: you finish with a piece you can post on ArtStation or show to a studio.

How does support work?+

Every student gets access to the school's Discord. The invite is in the welcome lesson, right after you enroll.

What if I'm not happy with the course?+

You have a 15-day money-back guarantee. Half of the course is already open during that period, so you can decide with the content in hand.
